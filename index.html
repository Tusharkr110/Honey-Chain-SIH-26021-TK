Questions about Honey Chain?

हनी चेन के बारे में अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Clear answers for every jar.

🔍
Honey Chain is India's National Honey Traceability portal operated under the KVIC Honey Mission (Ministry of MSME). It utilizes blockchain smart contracts, IoT hive scales, and NABL testing to guarantee botanical purity from beekeeper to consumer.
Every jar is assigned a unique cryptographic block hash on PostgreSQL & MongoDB ledgers. Scanning the QR reveals the exact harvest date, apiary GPS coordinate, moisture content (<20%), and independent NABL lab chemical analysis.
Once an Aadhaar-registered beekeeper logs a harvest that successfully passes the automated AI audit and KVIC nodal inspection, a ₹175/kg bonus is credited directly to their Aadhaar-linked bank account via PFMS.
Dual consensus requires validation from two independent sources before a batch is minted: (1) IoT Apiary scale & acoustic telemetry (confirming natural nectar flow without artificial feeding), and (2) NABL chemical lab test (confirming 0.0% C-4 corn/sugar adulteration).
Yes. Aadhaar numbers are cryptographic SHA-256 hashed and never displayed publicly. Public passports display only government Honey Mission IDs and district-level origin.
